About Design Dost

Design Dost is a free online poster-making tool that helps users create simple promotional and informational posters in Urdu and English. It is designed for people who want to prepare their own designs using a mobile phone or computer without needing advanced graphic design skills.

We created Design Dost with a practical purpose: to make basic poster designing easier for small businesses, shop owners, students, creators and individuals. Instead of starting every design from a blank page, users can choose a ready-made template and customize it according to their needs.

Why We Created Design Dost

Many small businesses and individuals need posters for sales, product promotions, announcements and social media updates. However, creating a design from scratch can take time and may require experience with professional design software.

Design Dost aims to simplify this process. It brings basic poster customization options together in one place, allowing users to add their own text, choose colours, include images and prepare a design for download.

Who Can Use Design Dost?

The tool is intended for a variety of everyday design needs, including:

  • Small businesses and shops: Create promotional posters, sale announcements and product advertisements.
  • Pharmacies: Prepare general promotional and health-awareness designs.
  • Restaurants: Make food promotions, menu highlights and special-offer posters.
  • Clothing stores: Design seasonal sale announcements and collection promotions.
  • Schools and academies: Create admission announcements, educational notices and event posters.
  • Social media creators: Prepare graphics for posts and status updates.
  • Individuals: Make simple posters for celebrations, invitations and personal announcements.

Features of Our Poster Maker

Urdu and English Text

Users can create designs using Urdu or English text. This is useful for businesses and audiences who prefer communicating in their local language or in English.

Ready-Made Templates

Users can select from available poster templates instead of designing every element from the beginning. Template content can then be customized for the intended purpose.

Multiple Poster Sizes

Different platforms and purposes require different image dimensions. Design Dost offers these size options:

  • Portrait: 1080 × 1350 pixels.
  • Square: 1080 × 1080 pixels.
  • Story / Status: 1080 × 1920 pixels.
  • Landscape: 1200 × 628 pixels.

Text and Design Customization

Users can add or change headings, descriptions, business names, offers and contact details. Available design options also allow users to customize colours and fonts.

Photo and Logo Options

Users can add their own photos and logos to personalize a poster. This can help make a promotional design more relevant to their business or event.

PNG Download

After completing a design, users can download the finished poster as a PNG image and use it for their intended purpose.

How Design Dost Works

The poster maker follows a simple process:

  1. Open the Design Dost homepage.
  2. Choose a poster category and available template.
  3. Select the poster size and language.
  4. Enter your heading, description, business name, offer or contact details.
  5. Customize the available colours, fonts and images.
  6. Preview your design and make any necessary changes.
  7. Download the finished poster as a PNG image.

Users can make changes to their content before downloading the final design. We recommend checking spelling, prices, contact details and other important information before sharing a poster.

Privacy and Uploaded Images

The poster editor processes uploaded photos and logos in the user's browser. These files are not uploaded to our website server through the poster editor.

Users should only upload images and logos they have permission to use. Avoid adding private or sensitive information to public promotional designs.

For more information about website data and privacy practices, please read our Privacy Policy.
