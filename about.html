About Design Dost

Welcome to Design Dost, a free online poster maker designed to help people create attractive posters quickly and easily using their mobile phones or computers.

Our tool is especially useful for small businesses, shop owners, pharmacies, restaurants, clothing stores, educational institutions and individuals who want to create simple promotional designs without needing professional graphic design software.

Our Purpose

Our goal is to make basic poster designing easier and more accessible. We aim to provide a simple tool where users can create their own designs, customize text and colours, add images and download their finished posters.

What You Can Do With Design Dost

  • Create business and promotional posters.
  • Design pharmacy and medical awareness posters.
  • Make food, restaurant and clothing advertisements.
  • Create education, event and celebration posters.
  • Choose between Urdu and English text.
  • Select different poster sizes for social media and other uses.
  • Customize headings, descriptions, colours and fonts.
  • Add your own photos and logos.
  • Download your finished design as a PNG image.

Easy Poster Designing

Design Dost is designed with a simple interface so that users can create posters without advanced design skills. Choose a template, customize its content, preview your design and download the final image.

The tool processes uploaded photos and logos in your browser. These files are not uploaded to our website server through the poster editor.

Free to Use

Design Dost is currently available as a free online tool. Users can access the poster maker without creating an account or making a payment.

Our Commitment

We aim to keep improving the website, make the tool easier to use and provide helpful information about poster designing. We also encourage users to review our website policies to understand how the website works.
