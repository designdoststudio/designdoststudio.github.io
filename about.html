About Design Dost

Welcome to Design Dost!

Design Dost is a free online poster-making tool created to help individuals, small businesses, shop owners and creators design attractive posters easily.

Our goal is to make graphic design simple and accessible for everyone, without requiring professional design skills or expensive software.

What You Can Do

  • Create posters using ready-made templates.
  • Write in Urdu and English.
  • Upload your own photos and business logos.
  • Choose colours, sizes and font styles.
  • Download your finished posters as PNG images.

Our Mission

Our mission is to provide a simple and accessible design experience for everyone, especially small businesses and individuals.
