Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 5, 2026

1. Introduction

Welcome to Design Dost. We respect your privacy and aim to explain clearly how information is handled when you use our free online Urdu and English poster maker.

2. Information You Provide

Design Dost allows users to enter text and upload photos or business logos to create posters. You may also voluntarily contact us by email.

Please avoid entering sensitive, confidential or personal information into your poster designs.

3. Uploaded Photos, Logos and Designs

Our poster maker processes uploaded photos and logos directly in your web browser using browser-based technology. The poster maker does not send these uploaded images to our own server.

Your poster is created on your device and can be downloaded as a PNG image. Design Dost does not currently provide user accounts or an online storage service for uploaded photos or finished posters.

Uploaded images are used while creating your design in the browser. The poster maker does not save these images as website content or provide a facility to store your designs on our servers.

4. Cookies and Similar Technologies

Cookies are small text files that may be stored in your browser when you visit a website. Third-party services may also use cookies, web beacons, IP addresses or similar technologies to provide services, measure activity and improve advertising.

Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve advertisements based on a user's prior visits to this website or other websites.

Google's use of advertising cookies enables Google and its partners to serve advertisements to users based on their visits to Design Dost and/or other websites on the Internet.

These technologies may be used to display relevant advertisements, measure advertising performance, limit repeated advertisements and improve advertising services.

6. Third-Party Services

Design Dost is hosted using GitHub Pages. GitHub and other third-party service providers may process technical information when visitors access our website, according to their own privacy policies.

Our website may contain links to external websites. We do not control those websites and are not responsible for their privacy practices or content.

7. Children's Privacy

Design Dost is a general-purpose poster-making tool and is not intended to knowingly collect personal information from children.

8. Data Security and Retention

Design Dost does not provide user accounts or online storage for poster designs.

Information you voluntarily send us by email may remain in our email records for as long as necessary to respond to your inquiry or for legitimate business purposes.

9. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when our services, advertising arrangements or applicable requirements change. Updates will be published on this page with a revised last-updated date.
