Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 5, 2026

Introduction

Welcome to Design Dost. We respect your privacy and aim to explain clearly how information is handled when you use our free online Urdu and English poster maker.

Information You Provide

Design Dost allows users to enter text and upload photos or business logos to create posters. You may also contact us voluntarily by email. Please avoid entering sensitive, confidential or personal information into your poster designs.

Uploaded Photos, Logos and Designs

Our poster maker processes uploaded photos and logos directly in your web browser using browser-based technology. These uploaded images are not sent to our own server by the poster maker.

Your poster is created on your device and can be downloaded as a PNG image. Design Dost does not currently provide user accounts or an online storage service for uploaded photos or finished posters.

Uploaded images are used while creating your design in the browser. The poster maker does not save these images as website content or provide a facility to store your designs on our servers.

Cookies and Advertising

Design Dost may use third-party advertising services, including Google AdSense, to display advertisements. When these services are enabled, advertising providers may use cookies or similar technologies to display, measure and improve advertisements.

Google may use information from websites and apps that use its services. The information may be handled according to Google's privacy policies and applicable settings.

Learn more about how Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services: How Google uses information from sites or apps .

You can read more about Google's advertising technologies: Google Advertising Policies .

You can manage your Google advertising preferences through Google My Ad Center . You can also manage cookies through your browser settings.

Third-Party Services

Design Dost is hosted using GitHub Pages. Third-party service providers may process technical information when visitors access our website, subject to their own policies.

Our website may contain links to external websites. We do not control those websites and are not responsible for their privacy practices or content.

Children's Privacy

Design Dost is a general-purpose poster-making tool and is not intended to knowingly collect personal information from children.

Data Security and Retention

Design Dost does not provide user accounts or online storage for poster designs. Information you choose to send us by email may remain in our email records for as long as necessary to respond to your inquiry or for legitimate business purposes.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when our services, advertising arrangements or applicable requirements change. Any updates will be published on this page with a revised last-updated date.

Contact Us

If you have questions, concerns or requests related to this Privacy Policy, please contact us through our Contact Us page.
