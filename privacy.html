Introduction

Welcome to Design Dost. We respect your privacy and aim to explain clearly how information is handled when you use our free online poster maker.

Information You Provide

Design Dost allows users to enter text and upload photos or business logos to create posters. Please avoid entering sensitive or confidential information into your designs.

Uploaded Photos, Logos and Designs

Our poster maker processes uploaded photos and logos directly in your web browser using browser-based technology. The poster maker does not send these uploaded images to our own server.

Your poster is created on your device and can be downloaded as a PNG image. Design Dost does not provide an account or online storage service for your uploaded photos or finished posters.

Uploaded images are used while creating your design in the browser. When you close or refresh the page, the poster maker does not retain those images as saved website content.
