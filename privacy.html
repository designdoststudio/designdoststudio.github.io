Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 20, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Design Dost handles information when you visit our website and use our free online Urdu and English poster maker. We aim to explain our practices in clear and understandable language.

1. Introduction

Design Dost is an online poster-making tool that allows visitors to create promotional and informational designs using available templates and editing options.

This website does not currently require users to create an account or pay a subscription to use the poster maker.

2. Information You Provide

When using Design Dost, you may voluntarily enter information into the poster editor, such as:

  • Headings and descriptions.
  • Business or brand names.
  • Promotional offers and prices.
  • Contact details that you choose to display on a poster.
  • Photos and logos selected from your device.

You may also voluntarily send us information when contacting us by email. The information you include in an email may be retained in our email records so that we can respond to your inquiry.

Please avoid entering passwords, financial information, confidential business information or other sensitive personal details into public poster designs.

3. Uploaded Photos, Logos and Poster Designs

The Design Dost poster editor processes uploaded photos and logos directly in your web browser. These files are not uploaded to our website server through the poster editor.

Your poster is prepared on your device and can be downloaded as a PNG image. Design Dost does not currently provide user accounts or an online storage service for uploaded photos or completed poster designs.

Images selected for editing are used by the browser-based poster editor while you create your design. The poster editor does not provide a facility to save these uploaded images to a Design Dost server.

When you download a poster, the resulting image is saved through your browser or device download process. You are responsible for managing downloaded files on your device.

4. Automatically Collected Technical Information

When visitors access a website, certain technical information may be processed by the website hosting provider or other service providers. Depending on the services involved, this may include:

  • IP address.
  • Browser type and version.
  • Device and operating system information.
  • Pages or resources requested.
  • Date and time of requests.
  • Technical information used for security, troubleshooting and service operation.

Design Dost is hosted using GitHub Pages. GitHub may process technical information when visitors access the website in accordance with its own policies and practices.

We do not operate a Design Dost user-account database or a server-based poster storage system.

5. Cookies and Similar Technologies

Cookies are small files that may be stored in a visitor's browser. Similar technologies, such as web beacons, pixels or local storage, may also be used by websites and third-party services.

These technologies may be used for purposes such as:

  • Operating and maintaining website services.
  • Security and fraud prevention.
  • Understanding website usage.
  • Advertising delivery and measurement.
  • Managing advertising preferences where supported.

Third-party services may use cookies or similar technologies when their features are active on a page. The use of these technologies depends on the services implemented and applicable settings.

You can manage or delete cookies through your browser settings. Blocking certain cookies may affect the operation of some third-party features.

6. Google AdSense and Advertising

Design Dost may use Google AdSense to display advertisements if advertising services are enabled on the website.

When Google advertising services are used, Google and its advertising partners may use cookies, web beacons, IP addresses or other identifiers to deliver, personalize, measure or improve advertisements, subject to applicable settings and policies.

Third-party vendors, including Google, may use cookies to serve advertisements based on a user's previous visits to this website or other websites.

Google's use of advertising cookies enables Google and its partners to serve advertisements to users based on their visits to Design Dost and/or other websites on the Internet.

These technologies may also be used for advertising measurement, frequency management, fraud prevention and reporting.

7. Advertising Consent and Regional Requirements

Where required by applicable law or Google policies, visitors may be asked to provide choices or consent regarding cookies, advertising technologies and the use of personal information for advertising purposes.

For visitors in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland, Google requires publishers serving personalized advertisements to use a Google-certified Consent Management Platform (CMP) that integrates with the IAB Europe Transparency and Consent Framework.

Visitors should review the available consent choices and advertising preference controls where presented. Applicable consent requirements must be followed when advertising services are enabled.

More information is available in Google's EU User Consent Policy.

8. Third-Party Services and External Links

Design Dost uses GitHub Pages for website hosting. Third-party providers may process technical information when visitors access their services.

Our website may also contain links to external websites, including Google services and other information resources. These external websites operate independently and have their own privacy policies and practices.

We do not control the privacy practices, content or security of external websites. Visitors should review the privacy policies of those services before using them.

9. How Information May Be Used

Information processed in connection with the website may be used by the relevant service provider or by us, where applicable, for purposes such as:

  • Providing and maintaining website functionality.
  • Responding to emails and user inquiries.
  • Identifying and troubleshooting technical problems.
  • Maintaining website security.
  • Operating advertising services when enabled.
  • Meeting applicable legal or policy requirements.

10. Children's Privacy

Design Dost is a general-purpose poster-making website and is not specifically directed at children under the age of 13.

We do not knowingly request children to provide personal information through a user registration system. If you believe a child has sent us personal information by email that should not have been provided, please contact us so the matter can be reviewed.

11. Data Security and Retention

We aim to use the available website and service features responsibly. However, no method of internet transmission or electronic storage can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

Design Dost does not provide user accounts or server-based storage for poster designs.

Information voluntarily sent to us by email may remain in our email records for as long as reasonably necessary to respond to the inquiry, maintain relevant correspondence or meet legitimate business and legal requirements.

Technical information handled by hosting providers and third-party services is subject to their respective retention policies.

12. Your Choices and Rights

You can choose what information to enter into your poster designs and whether to contact us by email.

You can also manage cookies through your browser settings and manage Google advertising preferences through the links provided in this policy.

Depending on your location and applicable law, you may have rights to request access to, correction of or deletion of certain personal information. You can contact us with a privacy-related request.

13. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when website features, third-party services, advertising arrangements or applicable requirements change.

Any changes will be published on this page with an updated revision date. Visitors are encouraged to review this page periodically.
