How to Create Urdu Posters on Mobile – A Complete Guide

Urdu posters are widely used by shops, businesses, educational institutions, restaurants and social media creators to share announcements and promotional messages with their audiences. A well-designed Urdu poster can make information easier to understand for people who prefer reading in Urdu.

You do not necessarily need a computer or professional graphic design software to create a simple Urdu poster. With a mobile phone, an internet connection and an online poster maker, you can create and download your own designs.

1. Decide What You Want to Communicate

Before starting your design, decide the purpose of your poster. A clear message helps you choose an appropriate template, heading and background.

Some common uses of Urdu posters include:

  • Shop sales and discount announcements.
  • Business opening and closing information.
  • Product and service promotions.
  • Educational and academy announcements.
  • Restaurant menus and food promotions.
  • Birthday, wedding and special occasion greetings.

2. Open an Online Poster Maker on Your Mobile

Open Design Dost in your mobile web browser. The website provides an online poster editor with different templates, poster sizes and customization options.

You can start with a suitable template instead of designing everything from the beginning. This can make the process simpler, especially for beginners.

3. Choose a Suitable Poster Template

Select a template according to the subject of your poster. For example, a business template may be suitable for a shop announcement, while a birthday or event template may work better for a personal celebration.

Keep the design relevant to your message. Avoid using too many decorative elements that may distract readers from the main information.

4. Write Your Urdu Text

The main heading should communicate the most important information. Keep sentences short and use familiar words so that readers can understand the message quickly.

For example, a shop promotion might use headings such as:

خصوصی ڈسکاؤنٹ آفر

آج ہی خریداری کریں

آپ کا اعتماد ہماری پہچان

You can add supporting information below the heading, such as product details, offer dates and contact information. Check all Urdu spelling before downloading your poster.

5. Check Urdu Text Direction and Appearance

Urdu is written from right to left. Therefore, text direction and alignment are important when creating an Urdu poster.

Before finalizing your design:

  • Check that Urdu words appear in the correct order.
  • Make sure letters are connected correctly.
  • Check punctuation and spacing.
  • Keep headings and paragraphs easy to read.
  • Preview the design on your mobile screen.
Important tip: Urdu text rendering can vary between browsers and devices. Always check the final preview before downloading or sharing your poster.

6. Select the Right Poster Size

Choose your poster size according to where you intend to share it. Design Dost provides four useful formats.

Portrait – 1080 × 1350

A portrait poster can be used for general promotional designs, business announcements and social media content.

Square – 1080 × 1080

A square format is useful for social media posts and compact promotional graphics.

Story or Status – 1080 × 1920

A vertical story design is suitable for mobile story formats and status updates.

Landscape – 1200 × 628

A landscape poster can be used for horizontal promotional graphics and online banners.

7. Customize Colours and Fonts

Colours can make your Urdu poster attractive and help important information stand out. Choose a background that provides good contrast with the text.

Use readable font styles for important information. Keep the number of colours and font styles limited so that your poster does not look overcrowded.

8. Upload Your Own Photo or Business Logo

A relevant photo or business logo can make your design more recognizable. Design Dost provides options to upload your own photo and logo.

For better results:

  • Choose a clear image with good quality.
  • Use a logo that belongs to you or that you have permission to use.
  • Keep important text visible.
  • Avoid stretching images out of proportion.
  • Use photos that are relevant to the poster topic.

9. Preview and Download Your Urdu Poster

After completing the design, carefully review the poster. Check the Urdu text, spelling, image placement, font size and overall layout.

When everything looks correct, use the PNG download option in Design Dost to save your finished poster.

Open the downloaded image on your phone and check it once more. You can then share it through messaging apps or social media platforms.

10. Common Urdu Poster Design Mistakes

Avoid these common problems when making Urdu posters:

  • Using very small Urdu text.
  • Ignoring right-to-left text alignment.
  • Using too many colours and font styles.
  • Adding long paragraphs to a small poster.
  • Using unclear or low-quality images.
  • Forgetting to check spelling before downloading.
  • Placing text too close to the edges.
