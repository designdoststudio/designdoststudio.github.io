How to Choose the Right Poster Size

Poster dimensions affect how a design appears on different platforms. A design prepared for a vertical mobile status may not fit a square social media post in the same way. Select the dimensions according to where you intend to share the image.

  • Portrait – 1080 × 1350: A vertical layout for promotional posts and product information.
  • Square – 1080 × 1080: A balanced layout for square social media graphics.
  • Story / Status – 1080 × 1920: A tall layout for mobile stories and status updates.
  • Landscape – 1200 × 628: A horizontal layout for wide promotional graphics and link previews.
Practical example: If a shop wants to share a discount announcement on WhatsApp Status, the Story / Status size is a suitable starting point. If the same shop wants a square graphic for a social media post, it can select the Square size instead.

How to Make a Business Poster Easier to Read

A promotional poster has limited space. The viewer should be able to understand the main offer without reading a long paragraph. Organize the information in order of importance.

  1. Start with one clear message: Decide whether the poster is announcing a sale, a new product, a service or an event.
  2. Write a short heading: Use a heading that tells the viewer what the poster is about.
  3. Make the offer specific: If you mention a discount, clearly state the amount and any relevant conditions.
  4. Use a relevant image: Add a product photo or logo only when it helps the viewer understand the message.
  5. Keep contact details readable: Check that the phone number, website or address is correct.
  6. Use contrasting colours: Text should be easy to read against the background.
  7. Review the final image: Check spelling, prices, alignment and contact details before downloading.

Creating Urdu Posters on a Mobile Phone

Urdu posters can help communicate with audiences who prefer reading information in Urdu. A clear Urdu design should use familiar words, readable text and a simple information structure.

  • Write the main message in a short and understandable sentence.
  • Use a font option that displays Urdu characters correctly on your device.
  • Avoid putting too many words into a small area.
  • Keep prices and phone numbers easy to identify.
  • Check the preview carefully for incorrect characters or spacing.

Design Dost includes an Urdu language option. Select it, enter your Urdu text and review the preview. The appearance of Urdu characters can vary between devices and browsers, so always inspect the downloaded image before sharing it.

Common Poster Design Mistakes to Avoid

  • Too much text: A poster filled with long paragraphs can be difficult to read quickly.
  • Unclear offer: A discount should include enough information to avoid confusion.
  • Low-contrast colours: Similar text and background colours can make important information hard to see.
  • Unrelated images: A photograph should support the message rather than distract from it.
  • Incorrect contact information: A typing mistake in a phone number can prevent customers from reaching the business.
  • Wrong dimensions: Select a size that suits the platform where the poster will be shared.
