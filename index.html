How to Use Design Dost

  1. Select your poster category.
  2. Choose a poster size for social media or printing.
  3. Select one of the available design templates.
  4. Enter your heading, business name, offer and contact details.
  5. Choose your preferred colours and optionally upload a photo or logo.
  6. Tap Update Poster Preview and then Download PNG Poster.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is Design Dost free?

Yes. You can use the poster maker without creating an account or paying a design fee.

Can I create Urdu posters?

Yes. Select Urdu from the language option and enter your Urdu text.

Can I add my own logo or photo?

Yes. You can optionally select a photo and a logo from your device.

How can I save my poster?

Tap the Download PNG Poster button to save your design as a PNG image.

Are my uploaded photos sent to the website?

The poster maker processes selected images in your browser. It does not upload them to the Design Dost server.
