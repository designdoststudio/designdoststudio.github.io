What Can You Contact Us About?

  • Technical problems: Report issues with opening the website, selecting templates, editing designs or downloading posters.
  • Suggestions: Share ideas for new templates, categories, features or improvements.
  • Urdu and English support: Tell us about text display, language or readability issues.
  • Website feedback: Share your experience and suggestions for making the website easier to use.
  • General questions: Ask questions related to Design Dost and its available features.
  • Content concerns: Contact us if you notice incorrect information or have a concern about material published on the website.

How to Write a Helpful Support Email

To help us understand your inquiry, please include the following details where relevant:

  1. Write a clear subject describing your issue.
  2. Mention which page or feature you were using.
  3. Explain what happened and what you expected to happen.
  4. Mention whether you were using a mobile phone or computer.
  5. If possible, include a screenshot of the problem. Do not include passwords or private information.

Providing these details can make it easier to understand and investigate a reported issue.

Suggestions and Feedback

About Design Dost

Design Dost is a free online poster maker that helps users create Urdu and English posters for business promotions, shops, pharmacies, restaurants, educational activities, social media and special occasions.

Users can choose available templates, customize text and colours, add their own photos or logos and download completed designs as PNG images.

Learn more about the website and its features on our About Design Dost page.
