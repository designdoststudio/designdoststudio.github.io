How to Create a Professional Business Poster Online for Free

A business poster is a simple way to communicate an offer, introduce a product, announce a service or share important information with customers. A well-organized poster helps people understand your message without reading a long description.

Small shops, pharmacies, restaurants, clothing stores, academies and online sellers can all use posters to present their products and services. You do not necessarily need a computer or professional graphic design experience to start.

In this guide, you will learn how to plan a business poster, choose an appropriate size, write useful promotional text, select colours and create a design using the free Design Dost online poster maker.

1. Understand the Purpose of Your Poster

Before designing anything, decide what you want your customers to understand. Every poster should have one main purpose. Trying to advertise several unrelated products, services and announcements in one design can make the message confusing.

Common reasons to create a business poster include:

  • Announcing a seasonal sale or special discount.
  • Introducing a new product.
  • Promoting a service offered by your business.
  • Sharing shop timings or holiday announcements.
  • Advertising a new branch or business opening.
  • Sharing delivery and contact information.
  • Promoting an event, class or special offer.
Practical tip: Write your main message in one sentence before opening a design tool. If you cannot explain the purpose simply, your poster may also become difficult for customers to understand.

2. Identify Your Target Audience

A poster should be designed for the people who are most likely to use your product or service. Think about what information those customers need before making a decision.

For example:

  • A local grocery shop: Customers may want to see product names, prices and the duration of a special offer.
  • A pharmacy: Customers may need information about store services, opening hours and contact details.
  • A restaurant: Customers may be interested in food photographs, menu items, prices and ordering information.
  • An academy: Students and parents may need course names, admission dates, class timings and contact details.
  • A clothing store: Customers may want to know about new arrivals, available collections and discount conditions.

When you understand your audience, it becomes easier to decide which information deserves the most space.

3. Choose the Correct Poster Size

Poster dimensions affect how your design appears on different screens and platforms. Choose a format according to where you intend to share the image.

Portrait Poster – 1080 × 1350 pixels

A vertical format suitable for general promotional graphics, product announcements and business posts.

Square Poster – 1080 × 1080 pixels

A square format useful for social media posts, product promotions and business announcements.

Story and Status Poster – 1080 × 1920 pixels

A tall vertical format for mobile stories and status updates. Keep important information away from the top and bottom edges so it remains easy to see.

Landscape Poster – 1200 × 628 pixels

A horizontal format that can be useful for online banners and certain promotional graphics.

Important: These are the four formats available in Design Dost. Image requirements can differ between platforms and may change over time. Check the latest publishing recommendations of the platform you use.

4. Plan the Layout Before Adding Text

A professional-looking poster does not need to contain many decorative elements. Its layout should make it clear which information customers should notice first.

A simple business poster can follow this order:

  1. Business name: Identify the shop or brand.
  2. Main heading: Explain the announcement or offer.
  3. Supporting information: Describe the product or service.
  4. Visual element: Add a relevant photo or logo.
  5. Action or contact: Tell customers how to respond.

The main heading should usually be more noticeable than supporting details. Keep enough empty space between sections so the design does not feel crowded.

Example of a Simple Poster Layout

ABC FASHION STORE

SEASONAL SALE

UP TO 30% OFF

Selected Clothing Collection

Visit Our Store Today

Contact: 0300-0000000

Fictional example. Replace all details with accurate information about your own business.

5. Write Clear and Accurate Poster Text

Customers usually look at a promotional poster for only a short time. Use short sentences and place the most important information near the main heading.

Depending on your purpose, include:

  • Business or shop name.
  • Main heading or announcement.
  • Product or service description.
  • Price or discount, when relevant.
  • Offer starting and ending dates, if applicable.
  • Contact number or ordering information, if needed.
  • Important conditions that customers should know.

Make Discount Information Specific

Avoid vague statements when advertising an offer. If a discount applies only to selected products, mention that clearly. If an offer has an expiry date, include it so customers know how long it is available.

Less Clear

Special Discount Available!

More Informative

WEEKEND SPECIAL OFFER

15% OFF on Selected Items

Valid Friday to Sunday

Sample wording only. Use genuine offer details.

7. Select a Template That Matches Your Business

A template provides a starting layout and can save time when creating a poster. Choose one that gives enough space for your heading, image and important details.

  • Business and Sale: Shop promotions and discounts.
  • Pharmacy and Medical: Pharmacy-related designs.
  • Food and Restaurant: Food and cafe promotions.
  • Clothing and Fashion: Boutique and clothing offers.
  • Education and Academy: Admissions and course announcements.
  • Events and Celebrations: Special occasions and announcements.
  • Real Estate: Property-related promotional designs.

Do not choose a template only because it has many decorative elements. A simple layout with readable text is often more useful than an overcrowded design.

8. Choose Colours That Make Text Easy to Read

Colours help establish the appearance of a business poster, but readability should come first. Text needs enough contrast against the background.

  • Use dark text on a light background or light text on a dark background.
  • Choose a small number of colours that work well together.
  • Use a noticeable colour for the main offer when appropriate.
  • Avoid placing detailed text over a busy photograph.
  • Keep the background from competing with the heading.

9. Select Readable Fonts

Fonts affect how quickly customers can read your information. Decorative styles may look attractive in a short heading but can become difficult to read in longer descriptions.

  • Use a clear font for prices, dates and contact details.
  • Keep the main heading larger than supporting text.
  • Avoid using too many different font styles.
  • Check that Urdu characters are displayed correctly.
  • Preview the poster at a smaller screen size.

10. Use Your Own Photos and Business Logo

A relevant image can help customers understand what a business is offering. A logo can also make a poster easier to associate with your shop or brand.

When selecting images:

  • Use photographs that you own or have permission to use.
  • Choose clear images related to the product or service.
  • Avoid stretching small images to very large sizes.
  • Keep important poster text visible.
  • Do not use another business's logo without permission.
  • Check that the uploaded image has not covered important details.

12. Create Posters in Urdu or English

Choose the language that your intended customers understand most comfortably. Urdu may be useful for local audiences, while English may be suitable for some professional and online business designs.

If you are creating an Urdu poster, pay particular attention to spelling, text direction and readability. Keep the wording natural rather than translating every English phrase word for word.

13. Check the Poster Before Publishing

A poster may look correct in the editing area but still contain a spelling mistake, incorrect price or poorly placed image. Always inspect the final downloaded image.

  • Is the business name spelled correctly?
  • Is the main heading easy to understand?
  • Are prices and discount percentages accurate?
  • Are offer dates and conditions clearly mentioned?
  • Is the phone number correct?
  • Can the text be read on a mobile screen?
  • Are all photographs and logos permitted for use?
  • Does the downloaded PNG show the complete design?

14. Common Business Poster Design Mistakes

Avoid these mistakes when preparing a promotional poster:

  • Adding too much information to one design.
  • Making the main offer smaller than unnecessary details.
  • Using background colours that reduce text visibility.
  • Choosing decorative fonts for long descriptions.
  • Using unrelated, blurry or stretched photographs.
  • Forgetting important conditions of a discount.
  • Showing incorrect prices or contact information.
  • Using photographs or logos without permission.
  • Publishing without checking the downloaded image.

15. Where Can You Share Your Business Poster?

After downloading your poster, you can share it through channels that are relevant to your business. Choose the image format according to the platform and check how the image appears after uploading.

  • WhatsApp Status: Share short offers and announcements with your contacts.
  • Facebook: Publish product promotions and business updates.
  • Instagram: Share suitable square or vertical promotional designs.
  • Business groups: Share relevant information where promotional posts are permitted.
  • Printed material: Prepare a suitable design for printing after checking its dimensions and image quality.

Avoid sending repeated unwanted promotional messages. Share useful and accurate information with people who are interested in your products or services.

16. Common Problems and Possible Solutions

My Poster Text Is Difficult to Read

Increase the text size, improve the contrast between the text and background, and remove unnecessary words. Check the poster on a smaller screen.

My Uploaded Photo Looks Blurry

Try using a clearer original photograph. Enlarging a very small image can make it appear blurry.

My Urdu Text Does Not Look Right

Check the spelling and direction in the poster preview. Adjust the text and inspect the complete design again before downloading.

My Downloaded Poster Looks Different

Open the saved PNG image on your phone and inspect its complete layout. Check the edges, text placement and uploaded images.

My Poster Looks Too Crowded

Remove information that is not essential to the main message. Increase the spacing between sections and keep only the most useful details.
